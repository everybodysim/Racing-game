• Three crossing heights: a flat 4-way where the lap loops over itself (figure-8), elevated-cross blocks where the deck runs over the live road, and sealed closed-top tunnels where the road drives over the tunnel. Tunnel tops are a 50/50 map preference — under-road crossings are always forced closed no matter what the map prefers.
• Pool driving sections: the lap dives into the pond via a pool ramp, drives underwater, and climbs out the other ramp. Ponds also appear as pure decoration.
• Thin-block sections: a wide stretch narrows onto a thin elevated-style road (buried supports at ground level, real thin walls up top), then widens back — both at ground level and up on elevated detours.
• Surfaces can land on elevated roads too; open-top tunnel pits stay clean because their surface world is carved away (nothing to sit on).
• Same seed + same toggles = identical track, byte for byte.
