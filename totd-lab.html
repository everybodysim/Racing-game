• Closed-top tunnels seal the pit with a roof — the surface world (even a live road tile) stays on top, so the generator happily runs the ground loop straight over a closed tunnel section. That's the road-over-tunnel crossing.
• Pool crossings replace a straight stretch of the loop with pool-slope → water → pool-slope: you dive into the pond, drive underwater, and climb out the other side.
• Tunnel detours search for crossing opportunities the same way elevated detours do — they can cross the ground loop multiple times.
• Same seed + same toggles = identical track, byte for byte. Change one toggle to see exactly what it adds.
